# QA v0.1
Original procedural forearm bracer for Ratibor. Tapered leather shell, two iron retaining bands, leather edges, bronze rivets.

- LOD0: 1116 vertices, 1632 triangles; LOD1: 334 vertices, 408 triangles.
- Generator execution in JS: valid vertex indices and one UV per vertex.
- Not verified: independent Node run, manifold topology, rendered mesh, UE5 import, fit to Ratibor rig, art floor PR #7.
- No texture maps; PBR scalar parameters only. Geometry WIP, not accepted production quality.
