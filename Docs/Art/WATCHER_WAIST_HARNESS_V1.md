# Watcher waist harness — original geometry WIP
**Scene role:** articulated hip armor for the Watcher in the Ninth Entrance sequence. Separate component to be fitted to existing torso, gauntlets and greaves in PR #13.
**Delivered:** 12 curved hip lamellae, two circumferential forged bands, 24 raised rivets, front crest; OBJ geometry with per-vertex UV draft and deterministic JS generator.
**Geometry:** 1,164 vertices, 1,164 UV coordinates, 1,940 triangles, 39 groups. Dimensions in metres, Z up. Mesh is rigid static WIP; no rig or weight painting.
**Rebuild:** `node Tools/Art/generate_watcher_waist_harness_v1.js Content/Art/Characters/WatcherWaistHarness/SM_Watcher_WaistHarness_v1.obj`
**Blockers:** fit against Watcher torso and cloak; check gaps and intersections in DCC; create production UV islands and PBR textures; rig lamellae, author collision and LODs; UE5 import and screenshot not yet verified.
**Art direction:** forged dark iron over flexible worn leather; ninefold ceremonial theme. No third-party assets. No claim of AAA or UE5-ready status.
