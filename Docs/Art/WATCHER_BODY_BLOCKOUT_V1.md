# Ninth Entrance Watcher — body blockout v1

Original procedural humanoid silhouette for A01 ninth entrance encounter, intended to accompany the separately committed ritual mask. 2.19 m tall, Z-up metres. Body, hood, sleeves, legs and layered cloak surfaces; intentionally simplified and non-manifold in places.

**Status:** art blockout, not skinned, no UV, PBR, facial geometry, retopology, animation or UE5 import. Do not label AAA or engine-ready. Cloth is static surface, not simulated. The mask placement/scale must be checked in UE5 by Astra before integration.

**Rebuild:** `python Tools/Art/generate_watcher_body.py`. Geometry should be compared with the committed OBJ. The open cloak surface and overlaps need redesign for production.
